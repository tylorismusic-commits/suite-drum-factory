The workflow

Shape the kit, not the menu.

The point is to stay in the musical decision. Source buttons sit on the kit; the mixer stays open; the whole cymbal group changes as one.

Swap the shells

Choose a source for the kick, the snare and each tom independently. A Monster kick with a DRS snare is a valid starting point.

Change the whole cymbal group

One Cymbals Source menu replaces China, crash, hi-hat, ride and splash together — with safe per-role fallback if a pack is incomplete.

Mix it like a kit

Per-piece strips, bleed, pan, inserts and metering. Route to the Mix bus or 18 stereo host buses when you want stem control.

Save a configuration

Store a shell/cymbal selection as a CONFIG. Call it back for a song, a session or a whole album.

Shape the tone

Eight-band console EQ, compressor, drive, transient shaper, limiter and clipper inserts live on every piece.

Play it your way

MIDI map, loop browser, one-shot layers, per-instrument tuning and an e-drum wizard are all in the same window.

Good questions

Before you download.

The short version: it is a Linux plugin, the factory pack is large, the plugin is open source and your music stays yours.

Will this work in my DAW?

It ships as VST3 and LV2 for Linux x86_64. It was built and tested on Ubuntu 24.04 and should work in modern Linux VST3/LV2 hosts. Windows and macOS builds are not included in this release.

Do I need to download all 15 GB?

Factory Complete is one 15 GB sample pack containing all five kits. The plugin itself is about 20 MB. If you only want the base kit, Factory Base is the lighter free edition.

How does the install work?

Unzip the plugin archive and run ./install.sh, or copy the VST3/LV2 bundles into your user plugin folders. Rescan in your DAW, then drag SuiteDrumFactory-Factory.suitefactory onto the plugin window. The pack can take several minutes to install.

Is the plugin really open source?

Yes. The plugin code is AGPL-3.0 and the exact source for this build is on GitHub. The samples are separate works under CC BY 4.0 and CC BY-SA 3.0; attribution and licence files are included in the download.

Can I use the drums in commercial music?

Yes. The sample licences permit commercial music use. Attribution is required when you redistribute the samples themselves; share-alike applies to modified sample libraries, not to songs you make with them.

Why pay what you want?

The minimum keeps the store running; paying more directly supports the recording, editing, conversion, testing and documentation. If the factory is useful in your work, you can pay what it is worth to you.
